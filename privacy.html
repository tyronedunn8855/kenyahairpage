Privacy

Last updated October 11, 2026

When you book with Kenya, this site keeps only what she needs to confirm your appointment. Here is what that is, who sees it and when it is deleted.

What you give us

When you send a booking request or tap the deposit button, the site saves:

  • Your name and phone number
  • The style, add-ons, date, time, how long it takes and the estimated total
  • Anything you type in the notes box
  • Whether you said you paid the deposit, and how you sent the request (text, Instagram or the deposit button)

Your text or Instagram message goes through your own phone apps, and their own privacy rules apply to it.

Who sees it

Only Kenya's account opens her booking list. Tyrone Dunn, who builds and runs this site, has access for support and repairs. Nobody sells, rents or shares your details for ads.

When a request comes in, Kenya's phone gets an alert with the day, time and style. The alert never shows your name.

Deposits

Deposits are paid through Stripe. Your card or Apple Pay details go to Stripe, never to this site. Stripe tells the site that a deposit was paid and the amount, so Kenya sees it next to your request.

How long we keep it

Booking requests are deleted automatically 90 days after the appointment date. To fix or delete your request sooner, text Kenya at (414) 388-1130.

On your device

  • Your booking in progress is saved in your browser until you close the tab, so a refresh or the trip to Stripe doesn't lose it.
  • A copy of the prices and photos is kept in your browser so the page loads faster next time.
  • If you turn motion off, your browser remembers that choice.

This site uses no cookies, no ad trackers and no tracking pixels.

Visit counts

The site counts how many visits it gets each day and how many people open the booking section. These are totals only. Nothing in them says who you are.

Services this site runs on

  • Vercel hosts the site. Like any web host, it keeps short-lived server logs, which include IP addresses.
  • Supabase stores the booking list, schedule, prices and photos, on servers in the United States.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr and cdnjs deliver the fonts and code libraries the page uses, so they see your IP address when the page loads.

Kids

This site is for adults booking for themselves or their children. Kids should have a parent or guardian book for them.

Questions

Text Kenya at (414) 388-1130 or message @doitfortae_ on Instagram. If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
