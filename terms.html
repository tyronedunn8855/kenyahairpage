Sending a request does not hold a time. Your appointment is confirmed only when Kenya texts you back. Open times on the calendar can change until then.

Each style lists its price and about how long it takes. Prices with a + are starting prices, and add-ons such as extra length or a design cost more, as shown when you pick them. Your total is an estimate until Kenya confirms it.

The deposit shown on the booking page goes toward the price of your service. The deposit, grace period, late fee and cancel rules are listed under Before you come on the home page, and they are part of these terms. Text Kenya before you pay if you have a question about them.
